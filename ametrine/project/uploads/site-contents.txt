"Rare Insight. Real Impact."
Ametrine is a rare gemstone created where amethyst and citrine meet. That natural convergence inspires our work. We operate at the intersection of evidence and strategy, accountability and learning, technical rigour and human purpose. The result is insight that is not merely interesting, but useful—insight that strengthens decisions, improves programmes and contributes to lasting impact.
Primary Audiences
International and national NGOs, foundations and civil-society organisations.
Bilateral and multilateral development partners and donor-funded programmes.
Research institutions, universities and professional networks.
Government Ministries, Departments, Agencies (MDAs) and Local Government Authorities(LGAs).
Private-sector and social-impact organisations seeking stronger evidence and performance systems.
Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL), research, data and programme professionals seeking training or career opportunities.

ABOUT AMETRINE
Our Story
Ametrine Consulting was established from a clear conviction: development organisations generate large volumes of information, but the real value of data emerges only when it is credible, understood and used. Ametrine brings together senior expertise in monitoring, evaluation, research, learning, strategic information, quality assurance and organisational strengthening to help clients close that gap.
Our experience has been shaped by work with government systems, donor agencies, international and national NGOs, civil-society organisations, health facilities, education institutions and community structures. We understand the realities behind the numbers: limited capacity, complex reporting requirements, fragmented systems, competing priorities and the need to demonstrate results without losing sight of the people a programme exists to serve.
Like the rare gemstone from which we take our name, Ametrine represents convergence. We unite quantitative and qualitative evidence; global standards and local knowledge; accountability and learning; analytical depth and clear communication. This balanced approach enables us to deliver “Rare Insight. Real Impact.”

To be a trusted regional hub for evidence-based solutions that strengthen institutions, improve programmes and contribute to better lives.

How We Work
Listen and frame the decision. We clarify the purpose, users, questions, context and practical constraints before selecting methods.
Co-design a fit-for-purpose approach. We align the methodology, sampling, tools, indicators and outputs with the decisions the evidence must inform.
Deliver with discipline. We use clear protocols, trained teams, field supervision, daily quality checks and documented version control.
Analyse for meaning. We triangulate sources, examine patterns and differences, test interpretations and distinguish evidence from inference.
Communicate for use. We translate findings into concise narratives, visuals, recommendations and implementation priorities.
Strengthen ownership. We validate findings, transfer skills and provide tools or roadmaps that client teams can sustain.

Service 1 — Research, Evaluation and Learning
Independent evidence must be credible enough to withstand scrutiny and practical enough to guide action. Ametrine designs and delivers mixed-methods studies that answer important programme and policy questions.

Policy, strategy and programme reviews.
Case studies, evidence synthesis and learning reviews.
Our Evaluation Approach
We begin by clarifying the intended use of the study and developing an evaluation matrix that links each question to indicators, sources, methods and analytical approaches. Depending on the assignment, we combine surveys, document review, key-informant interviews, focus groups, observation, participatory methods and secondary-data analysis. Sampling is designed to provide the appropriate level of representativeness and diversity while remaining feasible and ethical.
Analysis goes beyond description. We disaggregate findings, investigate variation, compare perspectives and triangulate evidence. 
Typical Deliverables
Inception report and evaluation matrix.
Sampling strategy and fieldwork plan.
English and Swahili data-collection tools.
Ethics, consent, safeguarding and data-protection protocols.
Clean datasets, codebooks and analysis syntax where agreed.
Draft and final reports with clear findings and recommendations.
Executive summaries, briefs, dashboards and presentation decks.
Validation workshops and management-response matrices.
When to Engage Us
Engage Ametrine when you need independent evidence for programme design, accountability, learning, strategic review, scale-up or close-out—and when the usefulness of the findings matters as much as methodological quality.

Data Quality Assurance (DQA)
We assess whether data are valid, reliable, complete, precise, timely and protected. DQA work may include indicator verification, source-document review, recounting, reporting-chain assessment, system and capacity review, risk scoring, corrective-action planning and follow-up. Our approach treats DQA as a learning and system-improvement process rather than a one-time compliance exercise.
Analysis and Visualisation
We translate data into clear, decision-ready products tailored to executive, technical and public audiences. Outputs may include interactive dashboards, scorecards, maps, infographics, automated charts, survey tables, qualitative themes and narrative briefs. We apply appropriate disaggregation and explain uncertainty, definitions and limitations.
Tools and Platforms
Data analysis: Excel, Google Sheets, SPSS, Stata, R, Python and SAS, selected according to the assignment.
Qualitative analysis: NVivo and ATLAS.ti.
Visualisation and business intelligence: Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio, Flourish, Infogram, Plotly, RAWGraphs and Qlik Sense.
Digital collection and information systems: KoboToolbox, DHIS2 and context-specific databases.
Web visualisation where required: D3.js and Chart.js.

Frequently Asked Questions
What types of organisations do you work with?
We work with government institutions, development partners, international and national NGOs, civil-society organisations, research institutions and mission-driven private-sector organisations.
Do you work outside Tanzania?
Ametrine is based in Tanzania and is available for assignments across East Africa and other contexts where our expertise and delivery model are appropriate. Regional engagements are scoped according to local partnerships, logistics and regulatory requirements.
Can you conduct a complete evaluation?
Yes. We can support the full cycle: inception, evaluation framework, sampling, ethics, tools, recruitment and training, fieldwork, quality assurance, analysis, reporting, validation and communication of findings.
Can you strengthen an existing MEL system rather than build a new one?
Yes. We review what already exists, retain what works and prioritise practical improvements. The aim is not to replace systems unnecessarily, but to make them clearer, more integrated and more useful.
Do you provide digital data collection and dashboards?
Yes. Support may include form design, submission testing, field monitoring, data cleaning, Power BI dashboards and other visual products. Technology is selected according to user needs, infrastructure, licences and sustainability.
How do you protect research participants and data?
We use consent procedures, confidentiality safeguards, role-based access, de-identification, secure storage and documented data-management practices. Assignments involving sensitive populations or topics include context-specific safeguarding and referral considerations.
Can you support ethical or IRB clearance?
Yes. We can prepare the methodological and ethical documentation needed for review and support the submission process. Formal approval remains the responsibility of the relevant authorised review body.
Do you recruit enumerators and MEL professionals?
Yes. We can recruit or support recruitment of short-term field teams and specialised MEL or data personnel, including competency-based technical assessment.
Can you train our team using our own programme materials?
Yes. Customised training is often the most effective. We can use the organisation’s results framework, indicators, tools or datasets—subject to confidentiality controls—to make learning immediately applicable.
How do we request a proposal?
Use the inquiry form or email info@ametrine.tz with the terms of reference or a short description of the need, expected deliverables, location, timeline and budget parameters where available. We will review the request and advise on the next step.
How long does an assignment take?
Duration depends on scope, geography, sample, approvals, stakeholder availability and deliverables. A clear term of reference enables us to propose a realistic schedule and level of effort.
Will you share examples of previous work?
Where client permission and confidentiality arrangements allow, we can share selected public reports, anonymised samples or references relevant to the assignment.
